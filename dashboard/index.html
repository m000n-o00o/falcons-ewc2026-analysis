How to read this

Rank orders the six teams by their value on each metric (1st = highest). A higher value is not always better: for example, more time at zero boost is not a strength.

Each opponent is measured only in its 4–6 games against Falcons, so this is a head-to-head view, not a tournament ranking.

Avg of 5 opponents is a simple mean of the five team averages, not weighted by games.

Descriptive statistics only. Opponent figures come only from their 4–6 games against Falcons. Higher is not always better. Full glossary, units and limits: About the data.

Glossary: the 12 metrics, per player per game (mean of the team's 3 players)

Limitations

Opponent figures come only from their games against Falcons (4 to 6 games each). The dashboard cannot rank teams across the whole tournament.
Falcons figures are averages over all 23 of its games; each opponent's figures are averages over its 4–6 games against Falcons.
23 games from 5 series; Falcons won 18 and lost 5. Games within a series are not independent.
Every metric is a per-player mean of the team's 3 players for one game, not a team total.
Effect size = (winners mean – losers mean) / pooled std, over all 46 team-games (both teams in every game, not Falcons only). 0.6 is a rough reference, not a significance test.
Avg of 5 opponents is a simple mean of the five team averages, not weighted by games.
Shots are closely tied to goals. Demos inflicted and taken mirror each other.
Speed (km/h) and distance (m) are converted from Unreal units, so they are approximate. Boost units follow Ballchasing's scale and are not confirmed (marked *).
